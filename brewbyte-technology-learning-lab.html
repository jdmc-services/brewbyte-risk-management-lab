00 · Your technology journey

Welcome to BrewByte Coffee

BrewByte is a fictional startup opening its first coffee shop. The founders understand coffee but need someone to help choose devices, set up checkout, protect customers, and decide when AI could be useful. That person is you.

Our business

One store, eight employees, a modest startup budget, and a plan to sell drinks in person and online.

Your role

Act as a junior technology advisor. Explain your decisions in everyday language, then use the correct IT terms.

Your mission

Help BrewByte open safely and grow responsibly. Every decision connects to a foundational computing concept.

How learning works: Read a concept, explore a diagram, make a decision, request a hint only when needed, and complete an original four-choice assessment. You can learn without entering real personal data.
01 · Understanding Tech Basics

Technology makes the coffee shop possible

Learning objectives: Recognize the computing cycle, distinguish major device categories, identify common components, practice digital citizenship, and describe where AI uses computing resources.

AI connection: A forecasting model still needs input data, processing hardware, memory, storage, and output. AI is an application of computing—not a replacement for the computing cycle.

02 · Data & Privacy

Customers trust BrewByte with information

The startup introduces online ordering and a loyalty program. Students must recognize identifiers, understand privacy practices, protect accounts, and distinguish secure communication from insecure communication.

AI connection: AI can help predict demand or summarize customer feedback, but combining records can also expose identities. AI systems differ in how they collect, store, and use prompts. Never assume a service automatically deletes or anonymizes information.

Data in transit

Information traveling between systems; HTTPS typically uses TLS to protect web connections.

Data at rest

Saved information on devices or in cloud storage; access controls and appropriate encryption may protect it.

Authentication

Checking a claimed identity. MFA combines different categories, such as a password and a security key.
