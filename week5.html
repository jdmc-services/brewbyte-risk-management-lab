Governance map

What tells BrewByte what it must or should do?

Laws, contracts, standards, and policies can influence one control, but they are not interchangeable.

External authority

Law or regulation

A rule issued by a governing authority. Qualified stakeholders determine applicability.

Business promise

Contract

An agreement that creates duties such as reporting timelines or service levels.

Recognized practice

Standard or framework

Structured requirements or guidance adopted and tailored through governance.

Internal direction

Policy and procedure

Policy sets expectations; procedures define repeatable work, owners, and evidence.
