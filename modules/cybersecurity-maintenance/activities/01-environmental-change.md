# What changed?

Suggested time: 10 minutes. Work individually or in pairs.

## Learning goal

Explain why a successful initial security review is insufficient after people, assets, and partners change.

## Evidence to open

- [business-changes.csv](../data/business-changes.csv)
- [asset-inventory-baseline.csv](../data/asset-inventory-baseline.csv)
- [asset-inventory-current.csv](../data/asset-inventory-current.csv)
- [access-review.csv](../data/access-review.csv)

## Your work

1. Identify at least five business or technology changes using EV and BB record IDs.
2. For each, name an affected asset or process, a plausible threat, and a weakness that needs attention.
3. Propose one monitoring or maintenance action, its owner, and evidence of completion.
4. Select two changes that deserve early attention and explain the business consequences.
5. Explain one risk caused by an external change even if BrewByte changes nothing internally.

## Deliverable

Complete [the response template](../templates/01-change-observations.md). Use record IDs to support each important claim. Keep the response in your own files; there is no automatic upload or grading.

## Discussion

What evidence could change your decision? Which assumption did you make that someone else should verify?
