# Capstone: one issue across five domains

Suggested time: 15 minutes. Use BB-005, F-001, BB-ADV-01, and CHG-105.

Write five short paragraphs answering these questions:

1. **External monitoring:** What warning applies, and why?
2. **Internal monitoring:** What version and settings do you observe, and how do they compare with the approved state?
3. **Planning and risk assessment:** What business service is at risk, what decisions need authority, and who owns the outcome?
4. **Vulnerability assessment and remediation:** What would you validate, change, and retest?
5. **Readiness and review:** What would you rehearse or review to prevent recurrence and improve response?

Finish with a 100-word update for BrewByte's owner. Include the decision needed, current uncertainty, immediate action, and evidence required for closure. Do not imply this warning proves the separate tabletop outage's cause.
