# Prioritize and verify fixes

Suggested time: 15 minutes. Work individually or in pairs.

## Learning goal

Prioritization needs context, and closure needs evidence.

## Evidence to open

- [vulnerability-findings.csv](../data/vulnerability-findings.csv)
- [asset-inventory-current.csv](../data/asset-inventory-current.csv)
- [access-review.csv](../data/access-review.csv)
- [vendor-advisories.md](../data/vendor-advisories.md)
- [SCENARIO.md](../SCENARIO.md)

## Your work

1. Review all seven findings. Separate confirmed open findings, an unconfirmed report, and an evidence-supported closure.
2. Select your first three actions and explain the ordering using exposure, business dependence, existing controls, and uncertainty.
3. Complete a remediation tracker with an owner, temporary control if needed, target date, permanent action, and verification evidence.
4. Specify how F-005 should be validated. Do not prescribe an unsupported product fix from a version-only suspicion.
5. Explain why F-007 should not re-enter the patch queue without new evidence.
6. Explain the difference between blocking exposure, repairing a defect, and formally accepting residual risk.

## Deliverable

Complete [the response template](../templates/05-remediation-tracker.md). Use record IDs to support each important claim. Keep the response in your own files; there is no automatic upload or grading.

## Discussion

What evidence could change your decision? Which assumption did you make that someone else should verify?
