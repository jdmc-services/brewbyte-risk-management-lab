# Rehearse an outage

Suggested time: 15 minutes. Work individually or in pairs.

## Learning goal

Rehearsals reveal whether people, procedures, and recovery evidence meet the business need.

## Evidence to open

- [SCENARIO.md](../SCENARIO.md)
- [incident-timeline.csv](../data/incident-timeline.csv)
- [backup-and-recovery.csv](../data/backup-and-recovery.csv)

## Your work

1. Walk through the simulated events in timestamp order. Assign a facilitator, technical lead, operations lead, and business decision maker.
2. Identify at least four readiness gaps and record a corrective owner and verification method for each.
3. Compare the estimated 120-minute recovery with the 60-minute RTO. Calculate the gap.
4. Compare the estimated 1,440-minute data age with the 60-minute RPO. Calculate the gap.
5. Explain why neither a successful backup job nor an old restore test proves current recovery capability.
6. Draft a short status update containing confirmed facts, unknowns, immediate actions, and the next update time. Do not claim the advisory caused the outage.

## Deliverable

Complete [the response template](../templates/07-readiness-review.md). Use record IDs to support each important claim. Keep the response in your own files; there is no automatic upload or grading.

## Discussion

What evidence could change your decision? Which assumption did you make that someone else should verify?
