Protect identities before granting access.

1 · Understand the concept

  • Identification names an identity; authentication verifies it; authorization limits what it can do.
  • MFA strengthens authentication, but it does not replace authorization.
  • Zero Trust continuously evaluates identity, device, context and access.

Technologies to explore: Microsoft Entra ID · Okta · Keycloak. These are examples; compare official documentation, pricing and limitations before selecting tools.

3 · BrewByte case study

A cashier needs to process sales but must never see payroll.

Act as BrewByte’s junior security analyst. Your recommendation must balance risk reduction, operational continuity, usability and auditability.

Policy

Write one enforceable requirement: who must do what, under which conditions, and who approves exceptions.

Procedure

Document the steps, responsible role, expected evidence, escalation path and review cadence.

Validation

Describe an authorized test that demonstrates the control works, plus one limitation or failure mode.
