1 · Understand the concept

  • Encryption protects confidentiality; hashing supports integrity checks.
  • Digital signatures can authenticate a signer and detect tampering.
  • Keys require access controls, rotation policies and incident procedures.

Technologies to explore: OpenSSL · HashiCorp Vault · Azure Key Vault. These are examples; compare official documentation, pricing and limitations before selecting tools.
