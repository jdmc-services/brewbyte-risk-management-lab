Firewalls & Network Segmentation

Keep guest traffic away from business systems.

All modulesCapstone

1 · Understand the concept

  • A firewall enforces traffic policy; its rules need documented owners and review.
  • Segmentation limits unnecessary paths between trust zones.
  • Deny-by-default is a safer starting point than unrestricted access.

Technologies to explore: Cisco · Palo Alto Networks · pfSense. These are examples; compare official documentation, pricing and limitations before selecting tools.
