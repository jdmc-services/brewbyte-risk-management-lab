Mission briefing

One company, multiple locations, growing risk

BrewByte now operates four stores, a regional office, and cloud-hosted ordering and inventory applications. The help desk receives an alert that an office laptop accessed an unusual external file-sharing service shortly before an inventory report was discovered outside its approved storage location.

The CIRT authorizes a limited examination of a fictional forensic image to establish what files were present and whether deleted images can be recovered. Your role is to document findings without claiming more than the evidence supports.

Business assets at risk

Employee laptop, inventory reports, customer ordering data, credentials, cloud services, and network connectivity.

Roles

Help desk records the alert; CIRT coordinates response; forensic analyst preserves evidence; management determines operational and legal escalation.

Important: This is an original browser-based training simulation, not a forensic examination of a real disk. All filenames, hashes, timestamps, records, and findings are fictional. The browser does not inspect your computer. No real customer or employee data is used.

Why hashes matter

A SHA-256 hash can help detect changes to a particular evidence object when compared against a trusted reference. A matching hash supports integrity, but does not establish who created a file, whether a file is malicious, or whether an employee is guilty.
