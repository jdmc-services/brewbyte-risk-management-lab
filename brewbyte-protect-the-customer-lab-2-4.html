Key ideas to remember

PII and data classification

Information that identifies or can be linked to a person deserves appropriate handling. Classification informs access, storage, and sharing decisions.

Data lifecycle

Collect → use → store → share when authorized → retain as required → securely dispose when no longer needed.

Security controls

Access control limits who can use data. Encryption protects data confidentiality. Backups help with recovery; they do not replace access control.

AI governance

Before sharing business data with an AI service, consider necessity, authorization, vendor terms, data retention, and output verification.
