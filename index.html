Do I need an account or previous experience?

No. The Computer Fundamentals pathway is designed for beginners, and the learning activities are free to access.

How is artificial intelligence included?

Lessons connect fundamental concepts to AI use cases, including processors, data, privacy, security, and responsible use. AI is an application of the fundamentals, not a substitute for learning them.

Will my progress transfer between devices?

Individual labs may save progress in your browser. Browser-local progress does not automatically synchronize across devices and can be lost when browser data is cleared. The main portal does not collect student information.

Is BrewByte a real business?

No. BrewByte Coffee and the instructional scenarios are fictional and designed for technology education.
