Key terms

Read these definitions before starting the lab. The distinctions determine whether a requirement belongs in an executive document, a mandatory standard, or an operating procedure.

8 minutes
Cybersecurity policy

Management's formal direction for protecting information and regulating the authorized use of technology.

Cybersecurity program policyCPP

The executive policy that establishes program scope, authority, responsibilities, and strategic direction.

Issue-specific policy

Rules governing one resource, technology, or behavior, such as email, personal devices, remote access, or AI use.

System-specific policy

Managerial or technical direction for configuring, operating, and maintaining a defined system.

Standards

Mandatory, measurable requirements that translate policy into consistent control expectations.

Procedures

Repeatable steps showing how authorized personnel perform a task or meet a requirement.

Guidelines

Recommended approaches that help people make sound decisions when more than one method may work.

Practices

Accepted examples of behavior or work that demonstrate how people commonly comply.

Access control listsACLs

Permission structures that specify which subjects may perform defined actions on systems, files, services, or other objects.

Configuration rules

Instructions that tell a system how to process traffic, content, data, requests, or security events.

BrewByte scenario

Assume the role of a junior cybersecurity analyst advising a fictional company.

3 minutes

Business profile

BrewByte Coffee operates six stores, employs 74 people, and uses cloud scheduling, online ordering, a customer loyalty platform, managed Wi-Fi, and point-of-sale terminals. A small managed service provider supports the technology environment.

Ownership wants clear policy before opening two new locations. No real company data appears in this exercise.

Current risk observations

  • Managers share one administrator account.
  • Guest and business devices use the same network.
  • Staff copy customer details into spreadsheets.
  • A vendor keeps remote access active continuously.
  • Employees use public AI tools without guidance.
