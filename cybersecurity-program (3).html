Project Management Challenge — Control the MFA Project

BrewByte approves a project to deploy MFA to all administrative accounts. Halfway through, leadership adds SSO, passwordless authentication, a VPN replacement, and a new identity provider without changing budget or schedule.

Executive Close

Question: BrewByte buys EDR, MFA, a firewall, and a SIEM. Does BrewByte now have a complete cybersecurity program?

Exit Ticket
